What it does

A clear record is the first thing coercive control takes, and the hardest thing to rebuild without help.

Bloom Again does three things: it holds what happened, it explains the patterns behind it, and it gives you somewhere to steady yourself when the day gets away from you.

None of it is a diagnosis. The app names behaviours, never people, and it says so plainly wherever it speaks.

Privacy is the product

Built for someone who may be watched.

That shaped the engineering, not only the wording. Four decisions matter more than the rest.

  • 1

    Encrypted before it leaves

    Entries and vault files are encrypted on your device. Add a passphrase and a recovery code, and a new phone can open them too.

  • 2

    Nothing in your browser history

    Links open inside the app, so reading a legal-aid or helpline page leaves no trace on the device — the kind that outlives uninstalling.

  • 3

    Start with no account at all

    Continue privately, with no email. Add one later if you want your entries to survive a new phone.

  • 4

    Off until you say otherwise

    Analytics, crash reporting, health and voice are each disabled until you turn them on. Export or delete everything, permanently.

Questions

Before you download.

Do I have to create an account?

No. Continue privately starts the app with no email and no password. The trade-off is real: an account that exists only on your phone cannot be recovered if the phone is lost — by you, or by us. You can add an email later from Profile → Secure your account.

Can anyone at Bloom Again read what I write?

No. Entries and vault files are encrypted on your device before they are stored, with a key that stays on the phone unless you add a passphrase to wrap it. No human reads your AI conversations either. The privacy policy sets out exactly what is collected and what never is.

Will it show up on a shared phone bill or a family account?

Purchases appear in the Apple account that paid for them, under the product name shown at checkout. If the device or the Apple account is shared with the person you are documenting, stay on the free tier and read the safety notes in Support first.

Is this therapy, or legal advice?

Neither. It is a documentation and self-support tool. It describes patterns consistent with documented behaviours; it does not diagnose anyone, and it is not a substitute for a professional. It never contacts anyone on your behalf.

Can I get my entries out again?

Yes. Export everything you have written at any time, and delete the account permanently from inside the app — including an account you started privately. How deletion works.

When is it coming to Android?

The build is done and on internal testing. Google requires a closed test to run before a new app can be published, so there is a fixed waiting period we cannot shorten. Email us and we will tell you the day it opens.
