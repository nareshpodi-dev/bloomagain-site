What it does

A clear record is the first thing coercive control takes, and the hardest thing to rebuild without help.

Bloom Again does three things: it holds what happened, it explains the patterns behind it, and it gives you somewhere to steady yourself when the day gets away from you.

None of it is a diagnosis. The app names behaviours, never people, and it says so plainly wherever it speaks.

Privacy is the product

Built for someone who may be watched.

That shaped the engineering, not only the wording. Three decisions matter more than the rest.

  • 1

    Encrypted before it leaves

    Entries and vault files are encrypted on your device. Add a passphrase and a recovery code, and a new phone can open them too.

  • 2

    Nothing in your browser history

    Links open inside the app, so reading a legal-aid or helpline page leaves no trace on the device — the kind that outlives uninstalling.

  • 3

    Start with no account at all

    Continue privately, with no email. Add one later if you want your entries to survive a new phone.

  • 4

    Off until you say otherwise

    Analytics, crash reporting, health and voice are each disabled until you turn them on. Export or delete everything, permanently.
