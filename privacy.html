BloomAgain Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026 Effective: 10 September 2026

The short version

You are reading this because BloomAgain holds some of the most private information a person can write down.

  • Everything you save — incidents, journal entries, recordings, files, mood and

health readings — is stored under your account only. No other user can see it.

  • We never sell your data. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone.
  • We never use your entries to advertise to you, and we don't let anyone else do that either.
  • When you use an AI feature, the text you're working on is sent to our AI provider

to generate a reply. Nothing else goes with it. You can use most of the app without ever touching an AI feature.

  • You can delete your account from inside the app, and that deletes your content.
  • We are not your therapist, your doctor, or your lawyer. BloomAgain helps you

recognise patterns — it does not diagnose anyone.

If you are in immediate danger, stop reading this and call 112. Other helplines are listed in §14.

The rest of this policy is the detail. We've tried to write it so you can actually read it.

2. Who this policy is for

BloomAgain is for adults, 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn that a child has created an account, we will delete it. If you believe a child is using BloomAgain, contact us at privacy@bloomagain.app.

If a child is in danger, Childline 1098 is a 24×7 helpline for children in distress, including children witnessing abuse at home.

We've split this by why it exists, because that matters more than a category name.

Bloom Again offers email sign-in and a guest session only. There is no Google or Apple sign-in, so no third-party identity provider receives anything about you, and we receive nothing from one.

3.2 Things you choose to put in

This is the heart of the app, and it is all optional. You decide what goes in.

  • Incident logs — what happened, when, how you felt, who was involved
  • Journal entries, gratitude notes, letters you write and don't send
  • Mood and trigger records
  • Self-assessment questionnaire answers (see §3.6)
  • Evidence vault contents — photos, documents, screenshots, audio files
  • Voice recordings you make in the app
  • Safety plan and safe contacts — names and numbers of people you'd reach for
  • Medication and sleep notes, if you use those features

Much of this will, by its nature, be sensitive: it may describe your health, your sex life, your religion or caste, your relationships, and criminal conduct by another person. Some of it may identify a third party — the person who harmed you, your children, your family. We treat all of it as sensitive, and §5 explains the rules we hold ourselves to.

3.3 Health and wearable data — only if you turn it on

If you connect Apple Health or Health Connect, BloomAgain reads:

  • Heart rate and heart rate variability
  • Sleep data
  • Related activity readings needed for the anxiety score

We use these to compute a personal anxiety awareness score — a comparison against your own recent baseline, so you can notice when your body is reacting before your mind catches up.

This is wellness information, not a medical measurement. It does not detect, diagnose, monitor, or treat any condition. It is often wrong. Caffeine, illness, exercise, a hot room, and a bad night's sleep all move these numbers. Never make a medical decision based on it.

If the app writes anything back to Apple Health (for example, a sleep log), we only write what you have asked it to write.

You can revoke health access at any time in your phone's system settings, and the app keeps working without it.

3.4 Voice recordings and speech-to-text

Two separate things, and they behave differently:

  • Voice recordings you make deliberately are audio files. They are uploaded

and stored under your account like any other file in the vault, and you can delete them.

  • Speech-to-text ("dictate this entry") uses your device's built-in speech

recognition. On iOS and Android this may mean audio is processed by Apple's or Google's speech service under their privacy policies, not ours. If that matters to you, type instead.

The microphone is never on unless you have started a recording or a dictation. BloomAgain does not listen in the background.

3.5 AI features

When you use the AI coach, pattern insights, letter drafting, or the weekly briefing, the app sends the relevant text to our AI provider through our own server. The AI provider's key never sits on your phone.

What is sent: the instruction that shapes the reply, plus the conversation or entry the feature is working on. What is not sent: your email, your name, your account ID, your health readings, your files, or anything from a feature you aren't currently using.

Engineering must confirm this per feature and correct this paragraph if it overstates — flag E-05.

Our AI provider is Anthropic (the Claude models), used under commercial API terms. We rely on those terms for retention limits and for the commitment not to train models on our API inputs. Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days of receipt or generation, except where a longer period is required to enforce their usage policy or comply with law - see privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866-how-long-do-you-store-personal-data — do not paraphrase this from memory.

3.6 Self-assessment questionnaires

Your answers are stored under your account and used to produce a reflection back to you.

A score is not a diagnosis. These questionnaires are structured prompts for self-reflection, not clinical instruments, and they say nothing about the mental state of the person who harmed you. Only a qualified professional who has assessed you can diagnose anything.

3.7 Payments

  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google through their app

stores, with RevenueCat managing entitlements. We receive an anonymous purchase identifier and your plan status. We never see your card number.

  • Practitioner bookings are paid through Razorpay. We receive

confirmation of the payment and the booking details. We never see your card or UPI credentials. Engineering to confirm the exact fields — flag E-06.

3.8 Technical and diagnostic data

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): device model, OS version, and a

stack trace when the app fails.

  • Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics): which screens are opened and which

features are used, so we know what to fix. We do not send the content of your entries to analytics.

  • Push notification token, if you enable reminders.
  • Device time zone, so reminders arrive at the right hour.

Analytics currently starts automatically. We are adding a setting to turn it off, and until then this policy must not claim otherwise — flag E-02. This section must be rewritten once that toggle ships.

3.9 What we deliberately do not collect

  • Precise location or GPS
  • Your contacts list, call log, or SMS
  • Your photo library beyond the specific images you pick
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Anything from other apps on your phone

5. What we will never do

We are writing these as commitments, not aspirations:

  1. We will never sell your personal data. Not under any definition of "sell",

and not under the broader "sell or share" definition used in California.

  1. **We will never use your entries, recordings, or health data to target

advertising** at you or anyone else.

  1. We will never publish or share the content of your entries without your

specific instruction, except where §8 (law) forces us to.

  1. We will never read your entries for curiosity. Staff access is limited to

named people, requires a documented reason, and is logged. We will not access your content to "look into" a support request unless you ask us to and tell us what to look at.

  1. We will never use your data to train an AI model, and we require the same

of our AI provider under its commercial terms.

7. How your data is protected

We want to be precise here rather than reassuring, because in your situation the difference matters.

What is true:

  • All traffic between your phone and our servers is encrypted in transit (TLS).
  • All content is encrypted at rest on Google's infrastructure.
  • Your data is scoped to your account ID. Security rules on the server reject

any request for another user's data — this is enforced on the server, not just hidden in the app.

  • Firebase App Check is enabled, so our backend rejects requests that don't

come from a genuine copy of the app.

  • You can turn on biometric / passcode lock so the app won't open without

your face or fingerprint.

  • Stealth features — an alternative app appearance and a panic gesture — are

available so the app is less identifiable on your phone.

  • The AI provider's key exists only on our server, never in the app, so it

can't be pulled out of the installed app.

What is not true, and we won't claim it is:

  • BloomAgain is not end-to-end encrypted. We hold the keys to the storage

your content sits in. That means a court order, a lawful demand, or a serious breach of our systems could expose it. If that risk is unacceptable for a particular piece of information, don't put it in the app.

The app currently displays "End-to-end encrypted" in the evidence vault. That string is wrong and must be changed before launch — flag E-01. This section is the accurate description.

What you should also know: the biggest risk to you is usually not our servers. It is someone with physical access to your unlocked phone. Turn on the app lock. Turn on a device passcode. Consider whether the person you're documenting can see your notifications.

No system is perfectly secure. If we ever suffer a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires we will notify the Data Protection Board of India without delay as the DPDP Act requires, tell affected users directly, and where the GDPR applies notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours.

8. When the law forces our hand

We may have to disclose data if we receive a valid, binding legal order — a court order, a warrant, or a lawful demand from an authority with jurisdiction.

Our commitments:

  • We will check that the demand is valid and narrow it where we can.
  • We will disclose the minimum required.
  • We will tell you unless we are legally prohibited from doing so.

We disclose data only when the law compels us to, and we refuse or narrow requests that are overbroad. Where we are permitted to tell you about a request, we will. We do not publish a transparency report yet; if that changes we will say so here.

11. Data leaving India

Some of our processors — Anthropic and RevenueCat in particular — operate outside India, so your data will be processed abroad.

For users in India, transfers are made in line with the DPDP Act and any restrictions the government notifies we do not make the app available where doing so would breach sanctions or export law.

For users in the EU/UK, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, together with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum where the UK GDPR applies.

12. If you're in California

You have the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information, and to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not sell or share your personal information, and we use sensitive personal information only to provide the features you asked for — so there is nothing to opt out of. You can still exercise every other right through §9, and we will not discriminate against you for doing so.

A US privacy lawyer should confirm this, and confirm whether any other state law applies.

13. This is not therapy, medicine, or legal advice

BloomAgain provides information, structure, and reflection tools. It does not provide healthcare, mental-health treatment, or legal representation.

  • The AI coach is software. It is not a counsellor and has no duty of care to you.
  • Pattern insights describe patterns consistent with coercive or controlling

behaviour. They are recognition, not diagnosis — of you or of anyone else.

  • Health readings are wellness information, not medical data.
  • The India legal information is general education about the law, not advice

about your situation. Laws change and courts interpret them differently. Only a qualified advocate who knows your facts can advise you.

See 03_ai_disclaimer.md for the full detail on the AI features.
